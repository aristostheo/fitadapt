# ADR 0001: Versioned deterministic baseline policy

## Status

Accepted for V0.1.

## Decision

FitAdapt will begin with deterministic, versioned calculations rather than a machine-learning
model. The initial REE formula is Mifflin-St Jeor (`mifflin_st_jeor_v1`). The required input
`sex_for_mifflin_equation` uses the binary values required by that equation. This field represents a
limitation of the selected formula, not a complete account of a person's physiology or gender.

TDEE will apply one of five named activity multipliers to REE. Each multiplier is a rough
population-level assumption; the future adaptive estimator is intended to improve on it.

The engine requires an explicit signed `requested_weekly_change_kg`. Cut values must be
negative and at least `-0.75%` of body weight per week; maintenance must be exactly zero; and
gain values must be positive and at most `+0.5%`. The daily calorie adjustment is calculated
using the versioned `7700_kcal_per_kg_v1` approximation and keeps the same sign.

The initial macro policy (`weight_based_macros_v1`) assigns protein at `1.6 g/kg/day`, fat at
no less than `0.6 g/kg/day`, and carbohydrates from remaining target calories. When protein
and fat alone exceed a target, calculation fails with an actionable macro-policy-infeasible
error. There is no universal calorie floor in V0.1.

## Consequences

Every public estimate will include formula and policy versions, its input assumptions, and
the component values used to reach it. The scope is adults ages 18–80, a product boundary
rather than an assertion of formula validity outside that range. This project does not provide
medical advice, and the energy-equivalent approximation does not imply linear real-world
weight change.
